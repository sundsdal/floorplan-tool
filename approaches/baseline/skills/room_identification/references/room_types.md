# Room Classification Guide

Use the visual cues below to classify each room identified in the walkthrough video.

## Kitchen
**Visual cues:** Countertops, sink, stove/oven, refrigerator, dishwasher, kitchen cabinets (upper and lower), backsplash, range hood.
**Typical size:** 6 - 15 m²
**Notes:** May be open-plan with living/dining area. If combined, classify the kitchen zone separately based on where counters and appliances are.

## Bathroom
**Visual cues:** Toilet, bathroom sink/vanity, bathtub or shower, tile walls/floor, mirror above sink, towel racks.
**Typical size:** 3 - 8 m²
**Subtypes:**
- Full bathroom: toilet + sink + bathtub/shower
- Half bathroom / powder room: toilet + sink only
- En-suite: attached to a bedroom

## Bedroom
**Visual cues:** Bed (or space clearly intended for one), closet or wardrobe, bedside tables, dressers. Often has a window and a door that closes.
**Typical size:** 9 - 20 m²
**Subtypes:**
- Master bedroom: largest, often with en-suite bathroom and walk-in closet
- Secondary bedroom: smaller, standard closet
- Child's room: may have bunk beds or smaller furniture

## Living Room
**Visual cues:** Sofa/couch, TV or entertainment center, coffee table, armchairs, bookshelves, large windows. Usually the largest open room.
**Typical size:** 15 - 30 m²
**Notes:** May be combined with dining area in open-plan layouts.

## Dining Room
**Visual cues:** Dining table with chairs, chandelier or pendant light above table, sideboard or buffet.
**Typical size:** 8 - 15 m²
**Notes:** Often adjacent to or open-plan with kitchen.

## Hallway
**Visual cues:** Narrow passage connecting rooms, multiple doors along walls, typically no furniture. Often no windows.
**Typical size:** 2 - 8 m² (narrow and elongated)
**Notes:** May have coat hooks, shoe storage near entry. Width is typically 0.9 - 1.2m.

## Closet
**Visual cues:** Small enclosed space, shelving or hanging rods, typically accessed through a single door.
**Typical size:** 1 - 4 m²
**Subtypes:**
- Walk-in closet: large enough to step into (2 - 4 m²)
- Reach-in closet: standard sliding or hinged door (0.5 - 1.5 m²)

## Laundry
**Visual cues:** Washing machine, dryer, laundry sink, ironing board, detergent storage.
**Typical size:** 2 - 6 m²
**Notes:** May be a dedicated room or a closet-sized alcove.

## Office / Study
**Visual cues:** Desk, office chair, computer/monitor, bookshelves, filing cabinets.
**Typical size:** 6 - 12 m²
**Notes:** May be a converted bedroom.

## Entryway / Foyer
**Visual cues:** Front door visible, coat rack or hooks, shoe storage, welcome mat, mirror.
**Typical size:** 2 - 6 m²
**Notes:** Transition space between exterior and interior.

## Garage
**Visual cues:** Garage door (large), concrete floor, car or space for car, tools, storage shelving.
**Typical size:** 15 - 40 m²
**Notes:** Not always present. May connect to house via a door.

## Basement
**Visual cues:** Below-grade access via stairs down, concrete walls or floor, utility equipment (water heater, furnace, electrical panel), low ceilings.
**Typical size:** 20 - 60 m²
**Notes:** May be finished (drywall, carpet) or unfinished (exposed concrete, pipes). May contain laundry area.

## Stairs
**Visual cues:** Staircase treads and risers, handrail or banister, landing areas, vertical transition between floors.
**Typical size:** 3 - 8 m²
**Notes:** Label the staircase footprint as a room. Includes the landing area if enclosed.

## Attic
**Visual cues:** Sloped ceiling following roof line, exposed beams or rafters, dormer windows, knee walls, storage areas.
**Typical size:** 10 - 40 m²
**Notes:** May be finished (bedroom, office) or unfinished (storage). If finished as another room type, classify by primary use.

## Ambiguous Spaces

If a space does not clearly match any type:
1. Look at what objects are present
2. Consider the room's size and position relative to other rooms
3. Note if it serves multiple functions (e.g., living/dining combo)
4. Default to the primary function based on the dominant furniture
